Innocent Shema’s seventh-minute header sealed the win for Silver Strikers after a tense afternoon. Shema’s goals were the first effort from the Area 47 side when Ernest Petro was brought down. Chinsisi Maonga’s free kick found Shema in the box, and the rest was history. Moyale Barracks started growing into the game in search of an equaliser that never came. In the 39th minute, captain Zebron Kalima was taken off after receiving a knock. In his place, Festus Duwe came on. We went to halftime with a slender lead. Come the second half, Moyale Barracks made some changes in search of a goal. Silver Strikers made a triple change, bringing Aubrey Khumbula, Andrew Joseph and Daah Rashid for Chinsisi Maonga, Emmanuel Allan and Charles Chipala. The changes brought rhythm as the Central Bankers created more scoring opportunities, but a second goal was never in sight. At 77 minutes, Team Manager Emmanuel Chipatala received a red card for complaining to the officiating panel about protecting goalkeeper George Chikooka. Ten minutes later, Silver Strikers piled pressure when Khumbula and Joseph both had chances that failed to materialise. The match ended in favour of the away side after regulation time. The Coach’s Reaction Interim Head Coach Patricio Kulemeka expressed excitement at winning the match, saying mission accomplished. “We came here with a mission, and the mission was to win and get maximum points. I am excited to win, and we are ready for our next opponent. Today we did not play our usual game as the environment did not permit us to do so; therefore, we twisted our plans to enable us to win”, said Kulemeka. Match Statistics A match that was so physical saw both sides attack each other’s final third. Silver Strikers registered 11 attempts with four on target whilst the hosts had 10 attempts and 5 were on target. The home side won most of the corners as they played seven corners against three by Silver Strikers. The Central Bankers received three yellow cards (Nickson Mwase, Charles Chipala, and Innocent Shema). Silver Strikers received a red card through Team Manager Emmanuel Chipatala. On the other end, Jimmy Viyuyi was the only one who went in the referee’s book. Man of the Match Ernest Petro-Silver Strikers Silver Strikers Starting XI George Chikooka (GK), Innocent Shema, Dan Sandukira, Lumbani Nyondo, Nickson Mwase, Yankho Billiat, Zebron Kalima, Ernest Petro, Charles Chipala, Chinsisi Maonga, Emmanuel Allan. Substitutes Festus Duwe (Zebron Kalima-39’), Aubrey Khumbula (Charles Chipala-63’), Daah Rashid (Chinsisi Maonga-63’), and Andrew Joseph (Emmanuel Allan-63’).
